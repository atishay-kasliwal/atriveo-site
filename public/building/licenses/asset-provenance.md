# Living-building asset provenance

The four GLB files, four 128px lighting atlases, eighty-household manifest and base shaders come from the owner's Phase 6/7 prototype at Desktop/blender. The prototype's provenance identifies these meshes, procedural materials, furniture arrangements, abstract artwork and screen appearances as workspace-created content, with no stock 3D assets, texture photographs, HDRIs or bundled fonts. This document does not assign ownership or relicense those assets.

Desktop and mobile WebP posters are derived locally from the filtered presentation geometry and original manifest's static initial appearance. They contain no branding, heading, statement or button text. All such content remains HTML.

Three.js 0.180.0 is the third-party browser 3D runtime. Its MIT notice is retained in three-MIT.txt. Astro, GSAP, Playwright and other existing site dependencies retain their package notices. Blender and offline authoring applications are not distributed with this integration.

The authority algorithm and compatibility fingerprint are preserved from Phase 7. No private world checkpoint, pending random choices, credentials or runtime storage is included in public assets.
