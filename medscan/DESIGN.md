# Medscan: follow the thread
Brief: a public landing page for the existing synthetic proof of concept, with a project entry in the Atriveo hub. The patient app stays at its existing hosted URL. No invented clinical outcomes, customers, claims or metrics.

## Concepts considered
| Territory | Concept and anchor | Signature / emotion / frame | Originality / fit / feasibility / portability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material | A folded report, grounded in generated PDF reports | Sheets settle into one report; calm; oversized paper composition | 3 / 4 / 5 / 3 |
| Process | Follow the thread from fragmented records through context to questions | A single teal path connects health and billing sheets; clarity; connected paper composition | 4 / 5 / 5 / 1 |
| Formal | Two columns converge into a conversation, grounded in the two source APIs | Columns merge; order; split typography | 4 / 4 / 4 / 2 |

Winner: follow the thread. It explains the actual API/context/report journey and stays meaningful across the home, about page and loose-thread 404.
Idea: separate health and billing records become a connected starting point for questions.
Metaphor: a thread through a patient's paperwork.
Signature: one SVG path connects paper records to a report, drawing in once, with a quiet connection pulse.
Emotion: calm, legible, trustworthy.
Motion verb: connect and settle.
Screenshot: oversized left-aligned three-line headline opposite connected, offset record sheets.

Palette: ground #f6f8f7; ink #173b35; muted #526660; line #d6e1dc; accent #00594f; field #def0e5. Teal preserves supplied Medscan branding.
Type: supplied self-hosted Inter (existing Medscan brand requirement), 400 body; system bold fallback; fluid display 48 to 88px with -0.065em tracking and 1.02 leading.
Grid: 12-column intent, hero 7/5 asymmetric, 64px desktop outer margin, 24px mobile. 4px base spacing, alternating tight strip, airy hero, list, dark statement, asymmetric explanation, poster finale.
Shape: paper has 8px corners; pill actions express connected continuation.
Motion: GSAP power3.out, one ticker drives Lenis; drawing once, slow opacity connection pulse; content visible without JavaScript. Reduced motion removes all movement. No loader or WebGL: access to information is the priority.

Composition:
Hero: [large text 7 columns][connected paper figure 5 columns]
How: [heading 5][three stacked workflow rows 7]
Context: [dark statement 6][question list 6]
Limits: [headline 6][honest description 6]
Finale: [large left text with action below]
About: [heading 4][explanation 8], repeated by actual architecture topic.
404: one loose-thread statement and a return action.

Genericness check: replaced the default app screenshot, feature card grid and invented patient metrics with an explicitly labeled paper/context illustration. No fabricated product screen. One visual signature, no decorative photography or anatomy figure required. No placeholder claims or assets.
